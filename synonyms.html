IELTS Synonyms Vocabulary

Vocabulary List

1. People

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Used to refer to individual human beings or collective groups within a society.

Synonyms: Individuals, The public

English Examples:

  • "Many individuals believe that the government should be solely responsible."
  • "The public often believes that the government should be solely responsible."

2. Important

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Expresses that a point or topic is crucial or essential for life or future success.

Synonyms: Crucial, Vital

English Examples:

  • "It is crucial for young people to learn a second language."
  • "It is vital for young people to learn a second language."

3. Good

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Indicates that something provides an advantage, help, or a direct positive effect.

Synonyms: Beneficial, Positive

English Examples:

  • "Spending time in nature has a beneficial effect on mental health."
  • "Spending time in nature has a positive effect on mental health."

4. Develop

Part of Speech: Verb

English Meaning / Context: Refers to making progress, growing, or bringing something new into existence gradually.

Synonyms: Cultivate, Foster

English Examples:

  • "Countries need to cultivate more sustainable sources of energy."
  • "Countries need to foster more sustainable sources of energy."

5. Increase

Part of Speech: Verb

English Meaning / Context: Describes rising data, initiating new programs, or boosting investments.

Synonyms: Boost, Raise

English Examples:

  • "The government plans to boost investment in the public transport system."
  • "The government plans to raise investment in the public transport system."

6. Government

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: The governing body of a nation or the individuals in power who create policies.

Synonyms: The authorities, The state

English Examples:

  • "The authorities are responsible for creating policies."
  • "The state is responsible for creating policies."

7. Society

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Refers to social communities or the general population of an area.

Synonyms: The community, The general populace

English Examples:

  • "Modern technology has had a profound impact on the community."
  • "Modern technology has had a profound impact on the general populace."

8. Technology

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Relates to new ideas, inventions, computers, applications, or digital tools.

Synonyms: Advancement of innovation, Digital tools

English Examples:

  • "The advancement of innovation has transformed the way we work."
  • "Digital tools have transformed the way we work and communicate."

9. Children / Youth

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Refers to young people or minors across childhood and teenage age groups.

Synonyms: The youth, Young people, Minors

English Examples:

  • "It is vital that the youth are taught about healthy eating habits."
  • "It is vital that minors are taught about healthy eating habits."

10. Students

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Individuals undergoing education, from primary pupils to third-level university scholars.

Synonyms: Learners, Pupils, Scholars

English Examples:

  • "Universities should provide more mental health support for their learners."
  • "Schools should provide more support for their pupils."
  • "The university provides funding for its most promising scholars."

11. Effective

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Describes a solution or action that yields desired, powerful, or successful results.

Synonyms: Productive, Potent

English Examples:

  • "Stricter penalties are a productive deterrent to crime."
  • "Stricter penalties are a potent deterrent to crime."

12. Necessary

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Indicates that an action or obligation is fundamental, essential, or urgent.

Synonyms: Essential, Imperative

English Examples:

  • "It is essential for individuals to take personal responsibility."
  • "It is imperative that individuals take personal responsibility."

13. Different

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Used for comparisons and highlighting distinct or varied approaches.

Synonyms: Various, Diverse

English Examples:

  • "There are various approaches to solving this problem."
  • "There are diverse approaches to solving this problem."

14. Work

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Refers to formal employment or a long-term professional career journey.

Synonyms: Employment, Career

English Examples:

  • "Finding fulfilling employment is a major goal."
  • "Building a fulfilling career is a major goal."

15. Companies

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Refers to commercial organizations, businesses, or large corporations.

Synonyms: Corporations, Businesses

English Examples:

  • "Corporations have a social responsibility."
  • "Businesses have a social responsibility."

16. Individual

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Formal alternative referring to a single person or citizen within a society.

Synonyms: Person, Citizen

English Examples:

  • "The rights of each person must be balanced."
  • "The rights of the citizen must be balanced."

17. Global

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Describes phenomena or issues operating on a worldwide or international scale.

Synonyms: Worldwide, International

English Examples:

  • "Climate change is a worldwide issue."
  • "Climate change is an international issue."

18. Modern

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Used to contrast current or present-day lifestyles with past traditions.

Synonyms: Contemporary, Present-day

English Examples:

  • "Contemporary lifestyles are often more sedentary."
  • "Present-day lifestyles are often more sedentary."

19. Many

Part of Speech: Quantifier / Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Used to denote a large quantity or numerous items.

Synonyms: Numerous, A large number of

English Examples:

  • "Numerous experts argue that automation will lead to job losses."
  • "A large number of experts argue that automation will lead to job losses."

20. Help

Part of Speech: Verb

English Meaning / Context: Academic verb meaning to render assistance or aid in development.

Synonyms: Assist, Aid

English Examples:

  • "Volunteering can assist students in developing a sense of responsibility."
  • "Volunteering can aid the development of a student's sense of responsibility."

21. Provide

Part of Speech: Verb

English Meaning / Context: The act of supplying a need or offering access to services or information.

Synonyms: Supply, Offer

English Examples:

  • "Libraries supply access to a vast range of information."
  • "Libraries offer access to a vast range of information."

22. Believe

Part of Speech: Verb

English Meaning / Context: Used to express an opinion by putting forward an argument or contention.

Synonyms: Argue, Contend

English Examples:

  • "I would argue that censorship in the media is sometimes justified."
  • "Some contend that censorship in the media is sometimes justified."

23. Create

Part of Speech: Verb

English Meaning / Context: The act of generating or establishing programs, jobs, or opportunities.

Synonyms: Generate, Establish

English Examples:

  • "The government should implement policies to generate more jobs."
  • "The government should establish a program to create more jobs."

24. Education

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Refers specifically to formal school instruction (schooling) or continuous personal development (learning).

Synonyms: Schooling, Learning

English Examples:

  • "Access to quality schooling is crucial for a nation's progress."
  • "Lifelong learning is crucial for personal and professional development."

25. Health

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Covers overall physical condition as well as mental and social well-being.

Synonyms: Well-being, Physical condition

English Examples:

  • "An individual's diet has a direct impact on their overall well-being."
  • "An individual's diet has a direct impact on their physical condition."

26. Family

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Refers to individuals living together in one home or a broader domestic unit.

Synonyms: The household, Domestic unit

English Examples:

  • "The average household now contains fewer people than it did 50 years ago."
  • "The domestic unit has changed significantly over the last 50 years."

27. Teachers

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Individuals involved in teaching, including formal educators, private tutors, or skill instructors.

Synonyms: Educators, Tutors, Instructors

English Examples:

  • "Good educators are essential for a positive learning environment."
  • "Many students hire private tutors to help them with difficult subjects."
  • "Driving instructors must be patient and clear."

28. Adults

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Formal or informal terms for individuals who have passed childhood.

Synonyms: Grown-ups, Mature individuals

English Examples:

  • "Many grown-ups are now returning to university."
  • "Mature individuals often have different learning needs compared to younger students."

29. Social

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Describes interactions relating to community life, public settings, or societal norms.

Synonyms: Communal, Societal, Public

English Examples:

  • "The rise of social media has impacted communal interaction."
  • "Societal norms have changed dramatically in recent decades."
  • "His behavior in a public setting was unacceptable."

30. Public

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Relates to state-funded transport/services, civic duties, or community resources.

Synonyms: State-funded / State-run, Civic, Community

English Examples:

  • "Investment in state-run transport is essential."
  • "Civic duty includes voting and respecting the law."
  • "The community swimming pool is open to everyone."

31. Local

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Specifies a particular neighborhood or community-based residential area.

Synonyms: Community-based, Neighborhood

English Examples:

  • "Supporting community-based businesses is beneficial."
  • "The neighborhood watch scheme helped to reduce crime."

32. Time

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Refers to non-work leisure hours or specific historical eras.

Synonyms: Leisure hours, Eras

English Examples:

  • "People today seem to have fewer leisure hours than in the past."
  • "In a bygone era, people had more free time."

33. Life

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Refers to human existence or specific modern ways of living and lifestyles.

Synonyms: Existence, Way of living / Lifestyles

English Examples:

  • "Technology has changed almost every aspect of modern existence."
  • "Technology has changed almost every aspect of modern lifestyles."

34. Experience

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Hands-on practical knowledge or specialized expertise gained over time.

Synonyms: Practical knowledge, Expertise

English Examples:

  • "Most employers look for candidates with practical knowledge."
  • "Most employers look for candidates with expertise in the field."

35. Skills

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Refers to practical abilities, workplace competencies, or academic prowess.

Synonyms: Ability / Abilities, Competencies, Prowess

English Examples:

  • "The education system should focus on teaching practical abilities."
  • "The education system should focus on developing core competencies."
  • "He was admired for his academic prowess."

36. Information

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: Refers to raw statistics and facts (data) or organized understanding (knowledge).

Synonyms: Data, Knowledge

English Examples:

  • "The internet allows for the instant sharing of data across the globe."
  • "The internet allows for the instant sharing of knowledge across the globe."

37. Community

Part of Speech: Noun

English Meaning / Context: A group of people or populace living in a region or society.

Synonyms: Society, Populace

English Examples:

  • "Public libraries are vital for a well functioning society."
  • "The local populace can benefit from public libraries."

38. Economic

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Relating to monetary policy, finance, or money management.

Synonyms: Financial, Monetary

English Examples:

  • "The government's main goal is to ensure financial stability."
  • "The central bank sets monetary policy such as interest rates."

39. Personal

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Affecting a single individual or belonging to private matters.

Synonyms: Individual, Private

English Examples:

  • "The choice to have children is a deeply individual decision."
  • "The choice to have children is a private matter."

40. National

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Extending across an entire nation (nationwide) or referring to domestic policy.

Synonyms: Nationwide, Domestic

English Examples:

  • "The government announced nationwide lockdowns."
  • "Domestic policy focuses on issues within the country."

41. Significant

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Large or substantial enough to be worthy of attention.

Synonyms: Substantial, Considerable

English Examples:

  • "There has been a substantial increase in the use of renewable energy."
  • "There has been a considerable increase in the use of renewable energy."

42. Possible

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Describes solutions that are practical (feasible) or capable of working successfully (viable).

Synonyms: Feasible, Viable

English Examples:

  • "It is feasible to reduce pollution if we take collective action."
  • "This is not a financially viable solution."

43. Available

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Describes whether a resource, product, or service can be obtained or accessed easily.

Synonyms: Accessible, Obtainable

English Examples:

  • "Not enough funding is accessible for arts and culture."
  • "Not enough funding is obtainable for arts and culture."

44. Successful

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Producing good results (fruitful) or achieving financial and business prosperity.

Synonyms: Fruitful, Prosperous

English Examples:

  • "The discussion was very fruitful and we came up with many new ideas."
  • "The business became very prosperous."

45. Popular / Ubiquitous

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Present everywhere in modern society (ubiquitous) or happening frequently.

Synonyms: Ubiquitous, Frequent

English Examples:

  • "Smartphones have become ubiquitous in modern society."
  • "Car accidents are a frequent occurrence on this road."

46. General

Part of Speech: Adjective

English Meaning / Context: Refers to a whole class considering everything (overall) or widespread agreement.

Synonyms: Overall, Widespread

English Examples:

  • "Overall opinion is that taxes are too high."
  • "There's widespread agreement that taxes are too high."

47. More

Part of Speech: Adverb / Adjective / Comparative

English Meaning / Context: Comparative term used to quantify nouns, indicating greater effort or additional funding.

Synonyms: Greater, Additional

English Examples:

  • "We need to put greater effort into protecting the environment."
